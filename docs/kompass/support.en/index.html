Kompass – Help and contact

Kompass supports people with a life-limiting illness and their relatives: clear information from palliativkompass.at, a personal diary, a medication list and appointments. The app is free, in German, has no account and no advertising, and everything stays on your device.

In an emergency call the Austrian ambulance 144 or the European emergency number 112. Health advice: 1450. In an emotional crisis: Telefonseelsorge 142. Kompass does not replace medical or nursing advice, and messages to the address below are not monitored around the clock.

Getting help

Write to support@nbsystems.dev. Please mention your device (iPhone or Android) and describe what you did and what happened; a screenshot without personal entries helps. Please do not send health data, passwords or photos of documents. There is no guaranteed response time.

Frequently asked questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Kompass works without signing in and without an internet connection.
  • Where are my entries? Only on your device. Nobody else, including the publisher, can see them. Details are in the privacy policy.
  • New phone: the diary is not backed up to iCloud and is not transferred to a new device; it starts empty there. To keep your entries, save or share the PDF first (“Als PDF für Arzt oder Team” in the diary, “Als PDF exportieren” in the medication list).
  • Locking the diary: turn on the lock in the diary under the gear (“Tagebuch-Einstellungen”). On the iPhone this needs a device passcode; the check uses Face ID or the passcode.
  • Reminders do not arrive: allow notifications for Kompass in your device settings. On Android, the “Alarms & reminders” permission also helps. The iPhone allows an app at most 64 scheduled reminders; medicines then take priority.
  • What is “Frag den Kompass”? A search in the articles: you ask a question in your own words and the app shows matching passages from the articles. It runs only on the device and does not make up answers, but it can be wrong and is labelled as a test version. It makes no diagnoses and gives no doses; for emergency, crisis and dosage questions it shows a note with the emergency numbers.
  • A result does not fit or an article contains an error: tap “Passt nicht? Frage per E-Mail melden” at the answer or write to support@nbsystems.dev. The articles come from palliativkompass.at; corrections arrive with an app update.
  • Deleting everything: “Alle Tagebuchdaten löschen” under the gear in the diary removes entries, medication list, appointments and reminders. Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device.
  • Does Kompass cost anything? No. No advertising, no in-app purchases, no subscription.
