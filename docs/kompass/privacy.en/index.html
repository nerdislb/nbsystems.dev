Kompass – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026 · applies to the app “Kompass” for iPhone (App Store, test versions via TestFlight) and for Android (installation file from this website), all versions from 0.1.0. The German version is the primary text.

Publisher and controller: Bernhard Slatinsek, Olga Rudel Zeynek Gasse 16, 8054 Graz, Austria, privacy@nbsystems.dev.

Brand: nbsystems is the controller's project and brand name, not a separate organization.

In short: Kompass has no user account and no server. Everything you enter stays on your device. The app makes no internet connection of its own, downloads nothing and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking services. The publisher does not receive what you enter. Data leaves the device only when you share something yourself.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your first name (optional), your saved articles, recently read articles, ticked checklist items and your own phone numbers for “Hilfe jetzt” (help now).
  • The diary: symptoms, vital signs (e.g. blood pressure, pulse, oxygen saturation), weight, bowel movements (frequency, consistency, colour, admixtures, amount, note, “no bowel movement”) and notes, each by day and time of day, and whom the diary is kept for (optional).
  • Your medication list with amounts, times, reminders, optionally a picture from the app's own selection (pictograms; the app does not access your photos) and the marks you set for taken or given doses. From these the app only shows counts and times, for example how often an as-needed medicine was given today.
  • Your appointments with place, note and reminder time.
  • Settings such as appearance and text size, whether the diary lock is on, and that you confirmed the notice on first start.

This includes health information. It is stored only in the app's private storage on your device and is not transferred to the publisher or to anyone else. The app does not evaluate your entries, makes no diagnoses and gives no recommendations.

The articles of the guide and topic pages come from palliativkompass.at and are used with the operator's permission. They are part of the app and are not downloaded.

Ask the Kompass (“Frag den Kompass”)

Your question is matched against the app's articles on the device only, also with the help of a small language model that is part of the app; it downloads nothing and sends nothing. Questions and results are shown only while you use the app, are not stored permanently and are not sent. Only if you choose “Frage per E-Mail melden” (report question by e-mail) does an e-mail draft open in your mail app, which you send or discard. The feature is experimental, can be wrong and does not replace medical or nursing advice.

Permissions

iPhone

  • Notifications: only for reminders you turn on yourself. The text names neither a medicine nor an appointment.
  • Face ID or passcode: only if you turn on the diary lock. iOS performs the check; the app only receives the result and stores no biometric data.
  • Calendar: no read access. When you add an appointment or a bowel-movement entry to your calendar, Kompass opens the iOS “New Event” screen with pre-filled details. It is saved only when you confirm. Kompass does not read your calendar.
  • Phone: “Anrufen” (call) in “Hilfe jetzt” hands the number to the Phone app; it is dialled only after you confirm.

Android

  • Notifications: only for reminders you turn on yourself, with the same neutral texts; marked as private on the lock screen.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms): so reminders arrive on time. Without it the app keeps working; reminders may then arrive up to an hour later.
  • Run at startup: to schedule reminders again after a restart or a time change.
  • Device lock and biometrics: only if you turn on the diary lock. Android performs the check; the app only receives the result. With the lock on, the app is hidden in the recent apps overview.
  • Calendar: no permission; as on the iPhone, the “new event” screen of your calendar app opens.
  • Internet: no permission.

Sharing only by you

You can share the diary or the medication list as a PDF and an appointment as a calendar file (.ics), as well as the link to an article. The file is created only when you ask for it, kept temporarily in the app's private cache and handed to the app of your choice through your device's share function. You decide who receives it and how it is handled there; from the hand-over on, the terms of the receiving app apply. A PDF may contain health information and is not encrypted.

Links and external sites

Links in articles (e.g. to palliativkompass.at, authorities or podcasts) open in your browser or the matching app. Kompass transmits nothing beyond the request your browser itself makes. Those sites have their own privacy policies. The website palliativkompass.at is not part of Kompass and not covered by this policy.

Backup and new devices

iPhone: the diary file (entries, medication list, appointments) is excluded from iCloud and computer backups. First name, own phone numbers, saved articles and settings are kept in the app's iOS settings and can be part of a device backup with Apple; you control this in the iPhone settings. Android: the app is set up so that Android neither backs up its data to a cloud nor transfers it to a new device. On a new device the diary therefore starts empty.

Deleting

  • In the diary, under the gear (“Tagebuch-Einstellungen”), “Alle Tagebuchdaten löschen” removes entries, medication list, appointments and reminders.
  • First name, saved articles and own phone numbers can be changed or removed in the app. On Android, “Settings → Apps → Kompass → Storage → Clear data” removes all of the app's data at once.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device.
  • Files you shared or saved, and copies held by recipients, remain. The publisher cannot delete them.

Data the publisher actually receives

  • E-mail: if you write to support@nbsystems.dev, privacy@nbsystems.dev or security@nbsystems.dev, your message and sender address are processed in the mailbox at the e-mail provider Purelymail in order to reply (legal basis: legitimate interest in answering, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Messages are deleted once they are no longer needed for the reply, at the latest after twelve months. Please do not send personal health data, passwords or photos of documents.
  • App Store and TestFlight: Apple processes installation, updates and test versions under its own terms. Apple gives the publisher at most aggregated statistics (e.g. number of installations) and – only if you agreed in the iPhone settings to share with app developers – crash reports. For TestFlight test versions the publisher also receives the feedback you send there yourself. None of this includes your entries in the app.
  • This website and the Android download: the website and the Android installation file are provided through GitHub (GitHub Pages and GitHub Releases); GitHub processes technical data such as the IP address (GitHub privacy statement). Legal basis: legitimate interest in providing them, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

GitHub and Purelymail are providers based in the USA; using them may involve transfers of data to the USA. According to GitHub, it bases such transfers on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Your rights and complaints

As the publisher does not receive your entries in the app, the publisher cannot evaluate, correct or hand them over; you have them on your device. For data the publisher processes in connection with your message, you can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and data portability and object, under the conditions of the GDPR. Contact: privacy@nbsystems.dev.

You can lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority, in particular the Austrian Data Protection Authority, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, Austria, dsb@dsb.gv.at.

Changes

If the app changes so that data could leave the device (e.g. an account or an internet feature), this policy will be updated beforehand and the change noted here. The version of 20 October 2026 applied to the test phase.
