Kompass Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026 · applies to the Android app “Kompass” during the closed test phase (version 0.1.0 and later) and, where stated below, to the iOS test version. The German version is the primary text.

Publisher/controller: Bernhard Slatinsek, Olga Rudel Zeynek Gasse 16, 8054 Graz, Austria.

Brand: nbsystems is the controller's project/brand name, not a separate organization.

In short: Kompass has no user account and no server. Everything you enter stays on your device. The app has no internet permission and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking services. The publisher does not receive what you enter. Data leaves the device only when you share something yourself.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your first name (optional), your saved list, recently read articles, checked items in checklists and your own phone numbers for “Hilfe jetzt” (help now).
  • The diary: symptoms, vital values (such as blood pressure, pulse, oxygen saturation, weight), stool (how often, consistency, blood, unusually pale) and notes, by day and time of day.
  • Your medication list with amounts, intake times, reminders and your marks of what was taken or given; the app only shows counts from them, for example how often an as-needed medicine was given.
  • Your appointments with place, note and reminder time.
  • Ask the Kompass: your questions are only matched on the device against the articles in the app. They are neither stored nor sent; only if you choose “report question by e-mail” yourself does an e-mail draft open, which you can send or discard.
  • Addition to “Ask the Kompass”: a small language model that is part of the app does the matching, on the device only. It downloads nothing and sends nothing.
  • Settings such as appearance (light/dark) and whether the diary lock is on.

This includes health information. It is stored only in the app's private storage on your device and is not transmitted to the publisher or to third parties. The app does not evaluate your entries and gives no recommendations.

The guide and topic pages come from palliativkompass.at and are used with the operator's permission. They are bundled in the app and are not downloaded.

Permissions

  • Notifications: only for reminders you turn on yourself. The text names neither a medication nor an appointment (“Reminder: please look at the medication list” / “An appointment is coming up”) and is marked private on the lock screen.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms): so that your reminders arrive on time. Without this permission the app keeps working; reminders may then arrive up to an hour late.
  • Receive device start: to schedule reminders again after a restart or a time change.
  • Device lock and biometrics: only if you turn on the diary lock. Android performs the check; the app only receives the result and stores no biometric data. While the lock is on, the app is hidden in the list of recently used apps.
  • Calendar: no permission. When you add an appointment to your calendar, Kompass opens your calendar app's “new event” screen with the details filled in. Nothing is saved there until you confirm. Kompass does not read your calendar.

Sharing happens only through you

You can share the diary or the medication list as a PDF and an appointment as a calendar file (.ics), and the link to an article. The file is created only when you trigger it, kept as a temporary copy in the app's private cache and handed over through Android's share function to the app you choose. You decide who receives it and how it is handled there; from the handover, the receiving app's terms apply. A PDF can contain health information and is not encrypted.

Links and external sites

Links in articles (for example to palliativkompass.at, authorities or podcasts) open in your browser or the matching app. Kompass transmits nothing other than the request your browser makes itself. Those sites have their own privacy terms. The website palliativkompass.at is not part of Kompass and is not covered by this policy.

No backup and no transfer

The app is configured so that Android neither backs up its data to a cloud nor transfers it to a new device. As a result, the app starts empty on a new device. The iOS test version follows the same basic idea (device only). There, the diary file (entries, medication list, appointments) is excluded from iCloud and computer backups. Your first name, your own phone numbers, saved list and other settings, however, live in the app settings of iOS and can be included in an Apple device backup, which you control in your device settings.

Deleting data

  • In the diary, under the gear icon (“Tagebuch-Einstellungen”), “Alle Tagebuchdaten löschen” removes entries, medication list, appointments and reminders.
  • First name, saved list, recently read articles, checklists, your own phone numbers and appearance are removed through the app's Android settings (“Apps → Kompass → Storage → Clear data”). That also removes all diary data.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of the app's data.
  • Files you shared or saved, and copies held by recipients, remain. The publisher cannot delete them.

Installation through Google Play and this website

If you install Kompass through Google Play, Google processes data about your Google account and the installation under its own terms. During the closed test phase the publisher manages a group of testers. The email address you use to join the group is visible to the publisher; the member list is not public. This website is served through GitHub Pages; GitHub processes technical data such as the IP address when you visit it (GitHub privacy).

Feedback

If you write to support@nbsystems.dev, your message and sender address are processed to answer you in the existing mailbox (Purelymail). Please do not send personal health data, passwords or photos of documents.

Children

The app is not aimed at children.

Your rights and complaints

Because the publisher does not receive your entries in the app, it cannot evaluate, correct or hand them out; you hold them yourself on your device. For data it does process in connection with your message or the tester group you may, under the conditions of the GDPR, request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability, and object. Contact: privacy@nbsystems.dev.

You can complain to a competent supervisory authority, including the Austrian Data Protection Authority, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, Austria, dsb@dsb.gv.at.

Changes

If the app changes so that data could leave the device (for example an account or an internet feature), this policy is updated beforehand and the change is noted here.
