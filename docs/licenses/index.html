Third-party components

Cutaway clock arithmetic

The Cutaway clock lineage includes time-derived ring geometry and clock arithmetic adapted from Dumidu's MIT-licensed Orbital Lock, reviewed at commit 6639304d250da02dc3e9771dec7a9c0fc0b87cb2.

O1 is now the only Home clock. The legacy Frame Cutaway implementation was removed during slimming; its attribution and MIT notice remain with the Cutaway lineage. The former full-screen ORBIT CENTER, ORBIT GREETER, and RADIAL HALF prototypes, their glass renderer, placement system, and radial presentation were moved to the separate nbclocklauncher prototype. Orbital Lock's session-lock, PAM, wake, idle, and authentication controllers are not used. nbOS remains an ordinary Android launcher and does not replace Android Keyguard.

The applicable MIT notice is reproduced in the Third-party MIT licenses section below.
