Zwischenzug – Help

Zwischenzug is chess in the look of a printed diagram: against the built-in computer or two players on one iPhone, with no account, no ads and fully offline.

Getting help

Write to support@nbsystems.dev. Include the app version, your iOS version and what you did. A screenshot helps. There is no guaranteed response time. TestFlight testers can also send feedback directly in TestFlight.

Frequently asked questions

  • How do I move? Tap a piece, then its target square, or drag the piece there. Possible moves are marked on the board.
  • Against the computer: In “New game” choose one of four levels (Beginner, Casual, Club, Strong) and your colour. The computer is our own engine and runs on the iPhone.
  • Two players with a clock (“Tisch”): Choose “Two players, one phone” and a time control, e.g. 5+3 (5 minutes, plus 3 seconds per move). Lay the iPhone flat between you; Black's half is turned around. The clock starts with White's first move and switches by itself after every move. Leaving the app pauses it. Running out of time loses, unless the opponent can no longer checkmate: then it is a draw.
  • Take back: Against the computer, “Take back” undoes your last move and the reply. With a clock it works only while the clock is paused.
  • Hint: “Hint” shows an engine move as an arrow on the board.
  • Analysis: “Analyse” checks every position of the game with the built-in engine. Inaccuracies (?!), mistakes (?) and blunders (??) appear in the score sheet; tap one to see the move the engine preferred. The judgement is our own engine's, not a grandmaster's.
  • Sharing a game: The share icon exports the score sheet as a PDF to print, or the game as a PGN file for other chess programs.
  • Piece sets: “Pieces” switches between the engraved standard set and the Bauhaus set, which shows how each piece moves.
  • Saving: The current game is saved after every move and comes back after a restart. It stays on the iPhone only.
  • Accessibility: VoiceOver reads every square (“e4, white knight”) and announces the computer's moves; the app follows the system text size.
  • Does it cost anything? No. No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription.
