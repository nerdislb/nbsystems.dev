Delete a SchichtPilot cloud account

You do not need an account for local planning. These instructions concern the optional SchichtPilot cloud account.

In the app

Open the cloud/account area in Tools while signed in, choose Delete cloud account, review the explanation and complete the final confirmation. Recent sign-in may be required. Do not treat an error or an unconfirmed result as successful deletion.

This removes the authentication account and sessions, associated active cloud data and sharing access, including membership/recipient references. Your local plan, Undo state, original backup and exported copies remain. Copies already held by recipients and provider logs/backups are not claimed instantly erased.

Without the app

If possible, send a request from the email address used for your SchichtPilot account to privacy@nbsystems.dev. Use the subject SchichtPilot account deletion and say that you want the SchichtPilot cloud account and its associated data deleted. You can also copy the address into any email service; you do not need to reinstall the app.

Do not include passwords, sign-in codes, roster attachments or identity documents. The operator must verify control of the account, normally by replying to its existing address; the sender address alone is not proof. If you no longer control that address, say so and ask for an appropriate verification route. The operator verifies your authority before deleting the account and will confirm the outcome or explain what is still needed. Sending the email alone does not execute deletion.

If you only want to remove particular data, say that clearly. Do not delete your entire account just to report a correction.
