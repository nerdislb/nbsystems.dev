SchichtPilot support

SchichtPilot helps you review your own shifts from photos/PDFs and maintain a local plan. The first beta is a closed pilot, not a publicly available store release.

Ask for help

Email support@nbsystems.dev. Include the app version, Android/iOS version, the step you took, what you expected and what happened instead. The link opens a draft; you decide whether to send it. No response-time guarantee is made during the pilot.

Do not attach real rosters, other people's details, passwords or sign-in codes. Use an invented example. Send security reports privately to security@nbsystems.dev.

Keep a recoverable plan

Before replacing or removing data, export a JSON backup using the app's backup/export tools and keep it somewhere you control. An image, PDF or ICS export is not a full recovery backup. Review an import before confirming it. Undo returns to the preceding committed local plan; it is not unlimited version history. An original migration backup is not a backup of every later edit.

Deleting your optional cloud account does not delete your local plan. Uninstalling or clearing app data can remove local plans and preferences. Exported files and system backups are managed separately. Keep a backup before changing installation/distribution channels; a differently signed Android build cannot update the current development build in place.

Beta limitations

  • Check every recognized row/date before saving. Recognition is not a guarantee of a correct roster.
  • Test alarms on your device and keep your established alarm until delivery and cancellation are verified. Permissions, system version and signed capabilities matter.
  • Widget support, especially the iOS shared container, still needs final distribution-signature acceptance.
  • Cloud is optional, invitation-controlled and requires an explicit selected-work upload. Membership alone does not share a calendar.
  • Open Tools to choose System language, Deutsch or English. The setting does not translate your own shift names or notes.
