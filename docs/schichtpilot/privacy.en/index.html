Planning without an account

You can plan shifts without creating an account. Your calendars, custom shift names, times, notes, import comparisons, Undo history and original migration backup are stored in the app's local storage. Photo/PDF recognition runs on your device. SchichtPilot does not upload source images or PDFs for recognition. Import only your own roster row; do not include patient information or other people's roster rows.

If you allow access to device calendars, the app reads events for display. It does not change those calendars or include their events in SchichtPilot cloud uploads or widgets. Alarm permissions let the app schedule your own shift alarms. Widgets use a local own-work snapshot and hide codes and times by default. Availability depends on the operating system and signed app capabilities.

Language and other app preferences are stored on the device. Android app-data cloud backup and device transfer are disabled. The current iOS app does not explicitly exclude its planning files from system backups: Apple/device backup settings may therefore retain a copy. This is separate from SchichtPilot's optional cloud service.

Optional cloud pilot

Cloud access is optional and invitation-controlled. When you sign in, your email address, authentication session and optional profile name are processed by the configured Supabase service. The pilot database is hosted in Frankfurt, EU. Purelymail delivers sign-in emails and hosts the support mailbox. Its operator, Add Rabbit LLC, is based in the United States and identifies AWS us-east-1 in Northern Virginia as its hosting location. The Frankfurt database location is not a claim that all processing stays in the EU.

Accounts are provisioned outside the app for invited participants; public self-service registration is not available. Android supports either an email code or email and password for an existing account. The password or entered code is sent over an encrypted connection to Supabase for authentication and is not saved in local plans, preferences or recreation state. Supabase handles password verification and authentication sessions; passwords are not stored there as plaintext passwords. The iOS pilot uses email-code sign-in.

Only a selected, owned work calendar is uploaded after you review and confirm it. The payload includes its work events, shift templates, reviewed import snapshots and deleted-event identifiers needed for synchronization. Personal calendars/events, foreign calendars, source images/PDFs, device-calendar events, local Undo history and the original recovery backup are excluded.

The selected work calendar may contain sickness entries. These and corresponding entries in its reviewed import history are also transmitted to and stored by Supabase when you confirm the upload. Subsequent recipient-side presentation does not change this upload.

Groups, membership, invitations and explicit calendar grants are used for optional sharing. Within a group, its name, profile names (or a neutral fallback), account IDs and memberships are transmitted to group members. Your profile name applies across all your groups. Joining a group alone does not grant access to a plan; these group details are visible separately.

Calendar grants cover individually confirmed recipients and the selected scope. Shared plans are read-only for recipients and include the calendar name, time zone and owner's account ID. Details grants may disclose titles and absence types including sickness; schedule views neutralize sickness/vacation to ABS, while availability views show unknown. New or rejoined members do not automatically receive calendar access.

Invitation codes expire and can be revoked; anyone holding a valid code may join its group. The app does not offer creation of new browser read links in this beta. Anonymous link reads are disabled in the verified pilot project, and no read links were stored at the 16 October 2026 check. Revocation cannot recall screenshots or exports already saved by another person.

Session credentials are protected using Android Keystore-backed storage or device-only iOS Keychain storage. They are not included in exported plan files. The apps do not include developer-operated advertising or analytics SDKs. This does not mean that cloud access produces no stored technical data.

Cloud requests from either app are handled by Supabase infrastructure with technical access logging. In the inspected Android pilot, these records were found to contain IP addresses, IP-derived location such as city, region and country, client information (User-Agent), a technical client-fingerprint field (JA4), user and session identifiers, requested endpoints, response status and response timings. These records outlast the immediate handling of a request. IP-derived location is not GPS tracking; a client fingerprint does not automatically identify a unique device. Account-linked endpoint records also reveal use of cloud functions. While signed in, the app refreshes cloud information periodically and when returning to the foreground, even without a calendar upload. Technical request records support operation, performance diagnosis and security/abuse handling; IP-derived location is treated here as security metadata, not a geographic audience-analysis feature. The records are not a precise count of manual taps. See below for retention.

Providers and international processing

Supabase provides authentication, the database and cloud APIs as a service provider. Its data processing addendum forms part of its service agreement and includes contractual international-transfer safeguards. Its infrastructure subprocessors include AWS and Cloudflare. Operational/support processing can involve Singapore and the United States even where database data is hosted in Frankfurt. The residency and transfer explanation distinguishes these flows.

Cloudflare distinguishes customer request logs processed for its customers from network/security data processed for its own purposes. This policy does not claim that every provider use is exclusively on the publisher's instructions. Purelymail support stated on 16 October 2026 that it does not provide Data Processing Agreements and referred to its general privacy/security documents. For the closed test phase, the publisher nevertheless continues to use Purelymail for sign-in emails and the support mailbox. There is consequently no processor agreement and no separate contractual safeguard for the transfer to the United States with Purelymail. The mail route will be reassessed before general publication.

Exports, support and this website

JSON backups and image/PDF/ICS exports create copies at a destination you choose. A chosen cloud drive, mail app or recipient then handles those copies under its own terms. Plan exports are not encrypted by SchichtPilot. Protect them and delete unwanted copies separately.

If you email support, the message, sender address and any attachments are processed to respond to your request through the existing Purelymail mailbox. Do not send roster files, passwords, sign-in codes or screenshots containing personal information.

This static website uses no analytics scripts, cookies or contact-form backend. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub receives connection information such as your IP address when you visit it. Email links open your mail app; they do not send a message automatically.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you remove it through the app, clear app data where supported, or uninstall the app. Source files, system backups and exports outside the app are not removed with it. Removing a cloud account also does not erase the local plan, Undo or original backup.

Confirmed cloud-account deletion removes the authentication account, sessions and associated active cloud data, including sharing access and membership/recipient references. Provider logs and backups are not claimed to disappear immediately. Supabase log retention depends on the service plan and configuration; the dashboard lookup window is not a guaranteed deletion deadline. Supabase currently documents up to seven daily Free-plan backups, without making this a verified project-specific maximum for every retained copy. No immediate individual deletion from all provider backups is promised. Purelymail's published policy describes diagnostic logs retained for 30 days and provider-account backup retention up to 30 days after closing that provider account; deleting a SchichtPilot account does not close the shared Purelymail service account or automatically delete support correspondence.

The publisher deletes support and privacy emails from the mailbox no later than six months after the matter is closed. For requests concerning your rights (such as access or erasure), a brief record without message content – date received, type of request and outcome – is kept for three years to demonstrate proper handling. Purelymail's 30-day diagnostic logs are separate from this. See Delete an account for the in-app route and the email request route.

Consent and withdrawal

Before a work calendar is uploaded, the app asks for an initially unchecked consent to cloud storage, plus a separate consent for any details disclosure to selected recipients. Local planning remains fully usable without consent. You can withdraw at any time under Manage cloud calendars: this removes the entire active calendar copy including import history, grants and read links; your local plan, Undo, account and other calendars/groups are kept. The current consent version and time are stored with the active copy/grant and deleted with it. A content-free account counter prevents stale uploads from arriving after a withdrawal; it remains until account deletion. Provider logs, backups and copies already exported are not erased immediately by this.

Older test versions without this consent step can no longer upload or share calendars. Calendar copies uploaded with them remain stored until you withdraw them or delete your account; earlier grants become readable again only after fresh confirmation, and earlier read links are revoked.

Legal bases

The publisher processes account access, ordinary calendar data and requested collaboration to provide the requested cloud service under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Proportionate service security and technical reliability rely on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f); support relies on Article 6(1)(b) or (f), as appropriate to the request. The legitimate interests are protecting accounts, investigating errors/abuse and maintaining a functioning service, not advertising or unrelated profiling. The brief record of handled data-subject requests relies on Article 6(1)(c) in conjunction with Article 5(2) GDPR.

Sickness entries are health data. Their cloud storage and any details disclosure to selected recipients rely on your explicit consent under Articles 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a) GDPR, separately for each purpose. You can withdraw it at any time as described above; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it.

Your rights

Subject to the applicable GDPR conditions, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction and data portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your situation. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal. These rights do not imply that every retained security record must always be erased immediately.

Contact privacy@nbsystems.dev. The operator must verify the requester's authority proportionately. Requests must be handled without undue delay and normally within one month; any legally permitted extension must be explained within the first month. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes or roster attachments.

You can complain to a competent supervisory authority, including the Austrian Data Protection Authority, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, Austria, dsb@dsb.gv.at. Its individual-rights guidance explains the applicable conditions.
