`tools/generate_nbos_topographic_wallpapers.py`. They contain no external
images, models, textures, fonts, or AI-generated image assets. Exact hashes and
provenance are recorded in
`app/src/main/assets/wallpapers-manifest.json` and the proposal source under

MIT license. The pinned source and its license are retained under
`wallpapers/sources/catppuccin/`. Catppuccin is used only to identify palette
compatibility; nbOS is not affiliated with or endorsed by Catppuccin.
